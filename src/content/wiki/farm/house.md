Character creation in Moonlight Peaks asks you to name three things: yourself, your farm, and the Hellcat companion that followed you out of your father's castle. From there you can pick skin tone, eye color (each eye can be set independently), hairstyle and color, facial details, and an outfit — with more clothing options unlocking as you progress.

Moonlight Peaks is not gender-locked, so romance and marriage options are available regardless of the character you build.

## Expanding the Homestead

As you clear more Cursed Soil and unlock buildings, your farm layout expands well beyond the starting plot. Prioritizing early automation (see [Crops](/wiki/crops/)) makes the expansion process much less repetitive, since manually watering a large spread-out farm every night eats into the limited hours your nocturnal schedule gives you.
