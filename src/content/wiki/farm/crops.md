Before planting anything, you'll need to clear debris from your gothic estate and prepare the Cursed Soil underneath. Seeds are purchased from Mara's witch shop in town, and most crops respond to more than just watering — several enchanted varieties have extra feeding or mana requirements.

## Example: The Gobbler

The Gobbler is a Summer-only magical crop sold by Luna for 350 coins. It takes six nights to mature, yields one Gobbler per plant, and needs to be watered with Aquaflux rather than plain water. Uniquely, the plant will also request two fish or Critters to feed on while it grows — the specific type requested varies from plant to plant, so it's worth stockpiling a variety of inexpensive catches before committing to a large planting.

| Crop | Season | Grow time | Special requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gobbler | Summer | 6 nights | Aquaflux water + 2 fish/Critters |

## Automating Your Farm

Long-term, the most efficient strategy is to automate watering with Gargoyle Sprinklers as soon as they're available. Applying Shadow Fertilizer alongside them keeps soil hydrated for two consecutive nights, which frees up your limited nightly energy (see [Nocturnal Schedule](/wiki/nocturnal-schedule/)) for potion-brewing, exploration, and building relationships in town instead of manual watering runs.
