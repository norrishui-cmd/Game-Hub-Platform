You begin Moonlight Peaks with a complete set of basic farming tools. Two items are held back at the start: the fishing net and fishing rod, both unlocked by completing early story quests, and your wand, which starts broken and gets repaired shortly after as your introduction to the game's magic system.

## Accessing Your Tools

All tools are available through the radial tool menu, opened with a single button (right bumper on controller, with an equivalent keyboard binding). This keeps tool-switching fast during a single night's farming or exploration run.
