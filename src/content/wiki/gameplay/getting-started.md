Moonlight Peaks begins with your character storming out of their father's home after a heated argument — your father happens to be Count Dracula. With nothing but a small companion creature (a "Hellcat" you get to name), you arrive in the town of Moonlight Peaks to start a new life among werewolves, witches, and mermaids.

## Your Starting Tools

You'll have a full set of basic farming tools right from the start. The only ones missing are the fishing net and fishing rod, which unlock later by completing story quests. Your wand also starts out broken — it gets repaired early on to introduce you to the game's magic system.

To access your tools at any time, open the radial tool menu (default: right bumper on controller, or the equivalent key binding on keyboard).

## The First Night

Right after stepping outside your new home for the first time, you'll find Orlock — an older, visibly hungover vampire — asleep on your lawn. Either interaction option will wake him, though poking him is the gentler of the two choices.

## Schedule Basics

Moonlight Peaks runs on a nocturnal schedule: your day starts at 6:00 PM and you need to be back home by 6:00 AM, or you'll start taking sun damage. See [Nocturnal Schedule](/wiki/nocturnal-schedule/) for the full breakdown, including how to eventually brew a potion that lets you go out safely during the day.
