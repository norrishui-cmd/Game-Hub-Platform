Unlike most farming sims, Moonlight Peaks does not let you play during the daytime by default — your vampire character wakes up at 6:00 PM and must return home by 6:00 AM. Staying out past dawn causes sunlight damage, and you'll wake up the following evening with reduced energy as a penalty.

## Sun-Shield Potion

Later in the game, you can brew a Sun-Shield Potion at your cauldron. Drinking it grants a limited window of safe daytime exploration — useful for reaching areas or NPCs that are otherwise only around during hours you can't normally access.

## Why the Schedule Matters for Planning

Because your active hours are compressed into a single overnight block, efficient routing between your farm, the witch shop, and town errands matters more than in a typical 24-hour farming sim. Automating early tasks (see [Crops](/wiki/crops/)) frees up energy for exploration, potion-brewing, and courting the town's residents rather than repetitive watering.
