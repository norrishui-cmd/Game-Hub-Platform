Orlock is one of the first residents you meet in Moonlight Peaks — quite literally on your doorstep, asleep on your lawn after what looks like a rough night out. You can wake him by poking him or by dousing him with water; poking is the gentler option and doesn't affect your relationship negatively.

## Romance

Orlock is one of the town's romanceable characters. As with the rest of the cast, Moonlight Peaks is not gender-locked, so his romance path is available regardless of the player character you've built.
